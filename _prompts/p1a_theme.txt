【_style_base(st) 实现要求】`st.theme_use('clam')` 在 apply_theme 里做。本函数配置:
- `st.configure('.', font=(FONT_FAMILY, 10), background=BG, foreground=FG)`(注意 '.' 是全局默认)
- 'TFrame': background=BG
- 'TLabel': background=BG, foreground=FG(并 map 无特殊状态)
- 'TButton': background=BG_PANEL, foreground=FG, bordercolor=BORDER, lightcolor=BORDER_LT, darkcolor=BORDER, focuscolor=ACCENT_DARK, padding=(12,5);map: background=[('pressed', ACCENT_DARK), ('active', '#24364a')], foreground=[('disabled', FG_FAINT)]
- 'Accent.TButton'(继承 TButton 的 map,单独 configure):background=ACCENT, foreground='#0b1220', bordercolor=ACCENT, focuscolor=ACCENT;map: background=[('pressed', '#3dd5f5'), ('active', '#7eeefc')]
- 'Green.TButton': background=GREEN, foreground='#0b1220', bordercolor=GREEN
- 'Danger.TButton': background=RED, foreground='#0b1220', bordercolor=RED
- 'TEntry': fieldbackground=BG_INPUT, foreground=FG, insertcolor=ACCENT, bordercolor=BORDER, lightcolor=BORDER, darkcolor=BORDER, selectbackground=ACCENT_DARK, selectforeground=FG;map: fieldbackground=[('readonly', BG_INPUT)], foreground=[('disabled', FG_FAINT)], bordercolor=[('focus', ACCENT)]
- 'TCombobox': fieldbackground=BG_INPUT, background=BG_PANEL, foreground=FG, arrowcolor=ACCENT, bordercolor=BORDER, selectbackground=ACCENT_DARK, selectforeground=FG;map 同上 focus 边框变 ACCENT
- 'TCheckbutton': background=BG, foreground=FG, focuscolor=BG;map: background=[('active', BG)], indicatorcolor 不用管
- 'TLabelframe': background=BG_PANEL, bordercolor=BORDER, lightcolor=BORDER, darkcolor=BORDER
- 'TLabelframe.Label': background=BG_PANEL, foreground=ACCENT

【_style_options(root)】用 root.option_add 设置:
- '*TCombobox*Listbox.background'=BG_INPUT,'*TCombobox*Listbox.foreground'=FG,'*TCombobox*Listbox.selectBackground'='#0e3a4a','*TCombobox*Listbox.selectForeground'=ACCENT,'*TCombobox*Listbox.font'=(FONT_FAMILY,10)
- '*Menu.background'=BG_PANEL,'*Menu.foreground'=FG,'*Menu.activeBackground'=ACCENT_DARK,'*Menu.activeForeground'='#0b1220','*Menu.borderWidth'=0

【apply_theme(root)】`st=ttk.Style(); st.theme_use('clam'); _style_base(st); _style_advanced(st); _style_options(root); root.configure(bg=BG)`(第 2 块会追加 _style_advanced 定义,先调用无妨)。
